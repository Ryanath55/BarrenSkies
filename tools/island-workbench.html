What this is. The same density-function maths the mod runs, ported to run in your browser: Minecraft's Perlin and normal noise, its cubic splines, and the clamped vertical gradients that turn a surface height into rock. Nothing here comes from Terralith — island shape is entirely the mod's own, which is why it ports cleanly.

What it isn't. The noise is statistically identical to the game's, but it isn't seeded from your world seed, so you'll see different islands rather than yours. Shapes, proportions and the numbers in the readout all carry over; a specific island won't.
